pollution

Pollution is the introduction of harmful or unwanted substances or energy into the environment, contaminating the air, water, and land and causing negative effects on human health, wildlife and ecosystems. Pollutants can be solids, liquids, gases, or energy like heat or noise. Common types include air pollution from vehicle exhaust, water pollution from industrial waste , andland pollution from litter and chemicals.
